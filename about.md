# I build things to find out if they’re worth building.

<p class="lead">I’m a product and strategy generalist who enjoys the early stages, when the problem is still taking shape and the answer has to be tested.</p>

Six years at a venture studio taught me how to take an idea from question mark to working answer: understand the customer, build an MVP, and make an evidence-based call on whether it is worth pursuing.

I’m currently an MBA candidate at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business, focused on product management, strategy, and entrepreneurship. I bring a mix of customer empathy, operational thinking, and enough technical fluency to build the tool when that is the most useful next step.

## The work is serious. The rest of life doesn’t have to be.

Outside of work, I organize a multi-sport tournament for friends, build games for them, and occasionally try to make people laugh on stage. It’s a useful reminder that good experiences come from paying attention to the people using them.

## A few things I value

- **Start with people.** Understand how someone works before deciding what to make.
- **Make the next step tangible.** A prototype, a test, or a clear recommendation can move an ambiguous problem forward.
- **Be honest about the signal.** Sometimes the most helpful answer is that an idea should not be built as planned.
