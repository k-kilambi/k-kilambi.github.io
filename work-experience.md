## Selected projects

<section class="project-detail" aria-labelledby="buzzlink">
  <p class="project-category">Customer discovery · Venture validation</p>
  <h3 id="buzzlink">BuzzLink</h3>
  <p>A hiring platform concept for electrical contractors needed evidence from the people expected to use it. I led field research across Jacksonville, Phoenix, and Nashville, including 60 contractor conversations and outreach to 17 trade schools.</p>
  <p>Contractors did not see a meaningful difference from existing options, and 0 of 60 joined after seeing the MVP. Based on that signal, I recommended not building BuzzLink as scoped, helping the client avoid investing in a product the target customers did not want.</p>
</section>

<section class="project-detail" aria-labelledby="deal-flow">
  <p class="project-category">Automation · AI · Operations</p>
  <h3 id="deal-flow">AI Deal Flow Pipeline</h3>
  <p>Launch Factory’s inbound pitch review involved manually reading free-form submissions and entering details by hand. I built a Make.com workflow that uses GPT to extract a consistent set of company, team, traction, customer, and fundraising details, then records them in Airtable.</p>
  <p>When required details are missing, the workflow drafts a follow-up. The aim is to make submissions easier to compare so pitch-writing polish is less likely to outweigh the business itself.</p>
</section>

<section class="project-detail" aria-labelledby="recruiting-crm">
  <p class="project-category">Product · Full stack · 0 to 1</p>
  <h3 id="recruiting-crm">Recruiting CRM</h3>
  <p>MBA recruiting spread applications, contacts, and outreach history across spreadsheets, email, and LinkedIn. I built a full-stack CRM that brings those pieces together, so an application, the people connected to it, and the outreach history can be viewed in context.</p>
  <p>I used the tool during my MBA job search. <a href="https://github.com/k-kilambi/recruiting-crm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">View the project repository <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>.</p>
</section>

<section class="project-detail" aria-labelledby="just-game">
  <p class="project-category">Community · Product · Full stack</p>
  <h3 id="just-game">Just Game Olympics</h3>
  <p>I organize a full-day, multi-sport competition for friends, now in its sixth year with up to 32 participants. As the event grew, I replaced manual brackets and score tracking with a custom web app: live leaderboard, matchups, game rules, and an admin panel for entering scores.</p>
  <p>The event grew from pen and paper, to a read-only spreadsheet view, to a live tool when the coordination friction made that worthwhile.</p>
</section>
